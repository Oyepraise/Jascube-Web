ABOUT THE CLASS

Understand the world of digital security.

Cybersecurity focuses on protecting computers, networks, applications and information from unauthorized access, attacks and other digital threats.

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN

Develop your security knowledge.

Explore essential concepts used to understand and protect modern digital environments.

01

Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Understand the basic principles of cybersecurity, digital security and common security challenges.

02

Network Security

Learn how networks are protected and how security controls help prevent unauthorized access.

03

Threats & Vulnerabilities

Understand common digital threats, vulnerabilities and the ways systems can be exposed to attacks.

04

Data Protection

Learn fundamental techniques for protecting sensitive information and maintaining data security.

05

Security Awareness

Develop practical awareness of security risks, safe digital practices and responsible technology use.

06

Incident Response

Understand the basic process of identifying, responding to and recovering from security incidents.

WHO IS THIS FOR?

Learn how to think securely.

This class is suitable for people who want to understand cybersecurity and develop a foundation for working with digital security technologies.
